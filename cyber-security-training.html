Program focus

Identity Governance & Administration (IGA)

The program focuses on advanced enterprise identity security, explicitly using Saviynt Enterprise Identity Cloud (EIC) and SailPoint (IdentityIQ & Identity Security Cloud) to teach students, technical professionals, or corporate teams how to govern human and machine identities across hybrid ecosystems.

1.Identity governanceDigital identities, life cycle management, user profiles, directory services, and role assignment.
2.Lifecycle automation (JML)Automated onboarding (Joiner), internal transfers (Mover), offboarding (Leaver), and dynamic birthright provisioning.
3.Access authorizationLeast privilege enforcement, role-based controls (RBAC), context-aware policies, and resource tracking.
4.Lifecycle auditingAutomated provisioning, dynamic deprovisioning, access reviews, SoD, compliance reporting, and audit logs.
Customized deliveryExamples, exercises, and technical depth can be adapted to role, seniority, industry, and organizational risk priorities.
